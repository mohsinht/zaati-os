# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to be respectful, specific, patient, and mindful that personal-data software carries real risk. Debate ideas with evidence. Do not pressure anyone to reveal private data to reproduce a bug.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, publishing another person's information, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, and retaliation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
